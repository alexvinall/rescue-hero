How to Play Rescue Hero

  1. Press 'Start Mission' to generate a mission to play. Press it again if you want to change the mission.
  2. Go and play it with your friends or grown ups! Be the hero, save the day and celebrate together!
  3. Complete the mission in Rescue Hero by pressing 'Mission Complete!'.
